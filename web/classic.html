FUEL & EMISSIONS ASSUMPTIONS

A transparent scenario model

Fuel use = sailing hours × modeled daily burn ÷ 24, plus anchorage consumption. Daily burn includes a 3 t/day hotel load and propulsion scaled by (speed / baseline speed)2.5.

CO₂ uses an illustrative factor of 3.114 t CO₂/t fuel. Vessel loading, currents, engine limits, and fuel grade require calibration before operational use.

FORECAST METHOD

Berth timing and uncertainty

Waiting time = max(0, berth opening − sailing duration). The recommendation selects a speed between 10 knots and baseline speed that targets the scenario opening.

The ±2 hour window is an illustrative uncertainty assumption, not a calibrated confidence interval. Arrival flexibility is used to flag plans that arrive beyond the berth opening.
